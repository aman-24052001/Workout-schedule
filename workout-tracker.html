Sets & Reps Rule

Main lifts 3×6–10 · secondary 2–3×8–15 · isolation 2–3×10–20 · core 2–3×10–15 · planks 2×30–45s.

Stop most sets with 1–3 clean reps left. When every set reaches the top of its rep range, add weight next week.
Protein target: 0.8–1g / lb of goal bodyweight, floor 150g/day.
Steps: find your 7-day average, +1,000/day until 7–10k, busy-week floor 6,000/day.
